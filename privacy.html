Plain-language privacy notice

Your information, handled with care.

This notice explains what happens when you use the quote-request form on the Happy Cleaning website.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

In brief: the website does not have a form database or send the information you type into the form to a Happy Cleaning server. When you submit the form, your browser prepares a WhatsApp message using the details you entered and opens WhatsApp so you can review it. You decide whether to send that message.

Information you may enter

The optional quote request asks for your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, type of cleaning, property type, and any extra details you choose to provide. Avoid including sensitive information that is not needed for a cleaning estimate.

How the quote form works

The form runs in your browser and prepares a message addressed to the business phone number linked on this website. It does not save form submissions in a website database. WhatsApp does not receive the message simply because the form opens it; you review the draft and choose whether to press Send. If you do send it, Happy Cleaning will receive the information in that message and may use it to respond to your request, prepare an estimate, coordinate service, and maintain relevant customer communications.

Third-party services

The site links to WhatsApp and loads fonts from Google Fonts and illustrative images from Unsplash. Opening an external service or loading its resources can cause your browser to communicate with that provider. Those providers handle data under their own terms and privacy notices. Review their policies before using those services.

Cookies and analytics

The current static website does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies and does not include a website analytics script. Your hosting provider and external resource providers may process technical request data as part of delivering their services. This should be rechecked if analytics, advertising, embedded tools, or other integrations are added later.

Retention and your choices

The website itself does not retain the form details. If you send a WhatsApp message or email, the information may remain in those services and in Happy Cleaning’s communications according to their applicable practices. You can choose not to use the form and contact the business using the published phone or email details instead.

Contact

For questions about a quote request or how the business handles information after you contact it, email happylimpezaservice@gmail.com or call (856) 870-0188.

Business review before launch

This is a concise website notice, not legal advice. Happy Cleaning should review and approve it before launch, especially if the business later adds booking software, payments, analytics, a customer database, or other data collection.
